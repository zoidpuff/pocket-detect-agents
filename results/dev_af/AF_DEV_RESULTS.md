# AF-mode development results (dev / train-OOF only; AF test set never read)

Owner: AF-mode developer. Final configuration and rationale: `docs/AF_MODE.md` (PI decision D8).
Reproduce everything: `scripts/af_dev_run_all.sh <base model> <tag>`, then `scripts/af_dev_strata.py` and
`scripts/af_dev_report.py`. The tables below are generated from the `results/dev_af/eval_*.csv` and
`strata_*.csv` files.

## Data and protocol
**AF dev.** AF models (AFDB v6) of the 278 Track A dev entries that have transferred sites (Track C2 labels); 607 sites, 192 of them ion sites.
- Site centre: centroid of the transferred ligand atoms.
- Site table: `af_dev_sites_all.tsv` (`scripts/af_dev_prep.py`).
- Dev proteins are mostly confident: the median fraction of residues with pLDDT < 50 is 1.1 %, and only 8 proteins are more than 30 % disordered.

**AF train, out of fold.** The AF models of all 2,844 train entries (6,238 sites).
- Points are scored by the cross-fitting fold model that did not see the entry's `cluster30`.
- Ranker comparisons use cluster-CV rankers trained on the same folds.
- The standard ranker itself is in-sample on this set, but that applies equally to every geometric variant.
- This set is used only to get 10× more statistical power for mechanism decisions.

**Metrics.** LIGYSIS protocol (`pocketdetect.site_eval`):
- DCC12 top-N+2 (`recall_topn2`, the O5 endpoint);
- DCA4 top-N+2 (`recall_ldca_topn2`);
- pooled P1K. Its depth is 1,000 pockets, which is about 3.6 pockets per protein on dev but 0.35 per protein on train-OOF.

Additional precision metrics:
- top-1 precision;
- top-(N+2) pocket precision;
- P@k with k = #proteins.

Hallucination: a pocket whose mean pLDDT is < 50 on the **unpruned** model and that matches no site at DCC ≤ 12 Å (`pocketdetect.af_eval`).

Statistics: cluster bootstrap over `cluster30`, B = 1000. All Δ values are paired.

## Key findings
1. **No-op.** With uniform pLDDT 95, 579 of 579 PDB-dev predictions are byte-identical to standard mode. This holds for prune+soft and for λ restricted to pLDDT < 90, on both base models.
2. **Hallucinations.**
   - Naive BURROW on AF models: 5.5 % (dev) and 6.6 % (train-OOF) of pockets are hallucinated. In proteins more than 30 % disordered the rate is 37 % (14 % of the top-N+2).
   - Pruning + soft occlusion: 0.0–0.3 %.
   - The pLDDT < 70 filter: 0 %.
3. **Recall: AF mode is equivalent to the pLDDT < 70 filter, not better.**
   - AF dev: +0.7 [−1.4, +2.8].
   - Train-OOF: −0.5 [−1.3, +0.2] (REF) and −0.7 [−1.4, +0.1] (def0).
   - Versus naive: dev +1.6 [−0.3, +3.8]; train-OOF +0.0.
4. **Controls.**
   - Matched-random pruning costs 2.5–3.1 pts (train-OOF, significant).
   - The oracle crop at pLDDT ≥ 70 gives no gain over prune+soft.
   - Sites losing ≥ 50 % of their residues to pruning: 1/607 (dev) and 8/6,238 (train).
5. **Side-chain tolerance λ and the weight curve.**
   - λ on all side chains destroys recall: −2.1 (λ 0.5) and −15.7 (λ 0.25) on dev. The point model was trained on full occlusion.
   - λ restricted to pLDDT < 90 side chains, and a stronger opacity curve, hurt the near-low-confidence stratum (−3.1 to −4.2).
   - A gentler curve (30/70) is neutral or slightly worse.
   - None is adopted.
6. **AF-aware ranker** (pLDDT pocket descriptors, AF+PDB rows): a trade-off.
   - Gains: pooled P1K +6.7 to +6.9, and P@k +7.7 in proteins more than 30 % disordered.
   - Cost: −3.0 [−5.2, −0.6] recall at sites near low confidence (REF base model; −0.8 [−3.2, +1.5] on the frozen model).
   - PDB-dev non-inferiority is inconclusive on the speed preset.
   - AF-only rankers fail non-inferiority (−2.2 [−3.9, −0.5]).
   - The inter-domain surrogate (`pldom`) adds nothing.
   - Status: optional `--af-ranker`, not the default.
7. **P2Rank on identical AF models** (AF dev):

   | method | DCC12 top-N+2 | DCA4 | P1K |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | P2Rank default | 53.0 | 37.9 | 32.9 |
   | P2Rank `-c alphafold` | 56.2 | 36.9 | 31.3 |
   | BURROW `--af-mode` | 66.9 | 57.8 | 51.6 |

   P2Rank on our pruned input, or with the pLDDT filter, changes by less than 0.5 pt.
8. **Context.** The PDB chains of the same 278 proteins, in standard mode, give 68.0 DCC12 and 59.8 DCA4. AF mode on the AF models (66.9 / 57.8) recovers almost all of it.
